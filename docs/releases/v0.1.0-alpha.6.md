Sixth alpha: fewer prompts.

## Both apps

- **Unlock once.** Mayi asks for Touch ID or your password when you open its window on the Mac, and for your fingerprint when you open it on the phone. After that, showing values, pairing a machine, adding a device and making a recovery key ask nothing more. It locks again when the Mac locks or sleeps, or when the phone's screen goes off or Mayi has been in the background for five minutes. Opening the phone app from a request notification does not ask, so approving stays one tap.
- **Level 4 is unchanged:** those secrets still ask on every use, as their level says.
- **The eye shows the current value.** When you edit a secret, its values are loaded (with no prompt while Mayi is unlocked), and the eye next to a value shows it. For a level 4 secret, the first press asks for its unlock.

## Updating

Quit Mayi on the Mac, then `brew upgrade --cask mayi`. Obtainium offers the phone update.
