Thirteenth alpha: a clearer Devices tab on the phone.

## Devices on the phone

- **Two sections, by what each does.** "Hold your vault" lists your phones, Macs and recovery key, which keep a copy of your secrets. "Ask for secrets" lists the agents on your Mac and your paired machines, which get only what their ACL allows and store nothing.
- **One + button adds any of them:** a device, a machine (a server or VM running agents), or a recovery key. Pairing a machine was easy to miss before.
- **Until you have a recovery key, the list says so**, and tapping the note creates one.
- **Revoked machines and removed devices are dimmed and listed last**, with a quiet label instead of red text that looked like the Revoke button. The Mac app shows revoked machines the same way.

## The status bar

The phone's status bar and Mayi's notifications show the Mayi logo instead of a padlock.

## Updating

Update the phone through Obtainium. On the Mac, quit Mayi, `brew upgrade --cask mayi`, and allow the keychain prompt when Mayi starts. If you are coming from alpha.11 or earlier, read the [alpha.12 notes](v0.1.0-alpha.12.md) first: update every device so that ACLs are enforced everywhere.
