Seventh alpha: fixes from testing on a real phone and MacBook.

## Fixed

- **A request answered from a sleeping phone was dropped.** Waking the phone (pulling down the notification) made Mayi think the requester had gone, and the request ended on both devices. The phone now ignores that interruption, and `mayi` asks a device again if it ever drops a request on its own.
- **Level 4 values on the Mac needed two presses of the eye.** One press now asks for Touch ID and shows the value.

## Reaching your devices

- **Addresses follow your network.** Each device publishes its addresses again when its network changes (a new Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Tailscale coming up) and before each sync, rather than only at start.
- **All addresses are tried at once,** the one that worked last first. At home the local network usually answers first, away Tailscale, and either can be off.
- **Virtual machine networks are left out** (Parallels, VMware, Docker), so nobody waits on addresses only this Mac's guests can reach.

## Activity

- A request answered on another device now shows as **Withdrawn** in grey, rather than as a red refusal.

## Updating

Quit Mayi on the Mac, then `brew upgrade --cask mayi`. Obtainium offers the phone update.
