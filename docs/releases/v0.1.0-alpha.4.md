Fourth alpha: requests find you on whichever device you have, with no push service, and you can read your secrets in the apps.

## Requests reach you without Google

- **No push service, Google's or anyone's.** A machine connects to your devices directly, over the same network or Tailscale. Firebase and the wake-up relay are gone.
- **A request waits for you** instead of failing at once: `mayi` keeps trying your devices for up to an hour (`--wait`), then gives you 15 minutes to answer (`--timeout`) once one of them shows the prompt. Exit code 69 means no device could be reached, 75 that nobody answered.
- **Every device can answer, and the one you are using is asked first:** a Mac unlocked with recent input, or a phone unlocked with its screen on. The others are asked 30 seconds later, and the first answer wins.
- **Agents on your Mac reach your phone too.** `mayi` on a Mac with the app pairs that Mac with your vault the first time it needs to. Devices, "Agents on this Mac can also ask your other devices" turns it off.
- **For agents:** `mayi` prints a line only when something changes, and the skill tells Claude Code to run long waits in the background. The MCP server reports progress while it waits.

## Phone

- **Stays reachable while it sleeps.** Setup asks to let Mayi run in the background (battery "Unrestricted"); a banner shows while it is off, with a link for phone makers that stop apps anyway. Mayi only listens, so it costs almost no battery. A check every 15 minutes restarts it if Android stopped it.
- **No Google services needed.** The QR scanner now uses the camera directly; it asks for camera permission the first time.

## Both apps

- **See and edit your secrets.** Show Values on the Mac, "Show values" on the phone: after Touch ID, your password or a fingerprint, the values appear, ready to edit or copy. A copied value is cleared from the clipboard after a minute. Each view is listed in Activity.
- **An orange dot marks a secret that exists on only one device**, so it is not lost with that device unnoticed. It goes once another of your devices has synced it.
- The phone can now edit secrets that hold several fields.

## Updating

Update every device: quit Mayi on the Mac, then `brew upgrade --cask mayi`; Obtainium offers the phone update; on other machines, `brew upgrade mayi-cli`. On the phone, allow Mayi in the background when it asks. Older versions still talk to this one.
