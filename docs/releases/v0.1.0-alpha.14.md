Fourteenth alpha: Exit on the phone, and its version in sight.

## The phone

- **Exit, in Mayi's notification, turns it off until you open it again.** Mayi stops answering requests and syncing, and does not come back by itself, after a restart of the phone either. Opening Mayi serves again as before.
- **The version is shown at the bottom of Devices** ("Mayi 0.1.0-alpha.14"). On the Mac it is in the menu bar menu, above Quit.

## Updating

Update the phone through Obtainium. Nothing changed on the Mac or in `mayi` since alpha.13 apart from the version number. If you are coming from alpha.11 or earlier, read the [alpha.12 notes](v0.1.0-alpha.12.md) first: update every device so that ACLs are enforced everywhere.
